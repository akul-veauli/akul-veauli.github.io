Introduction

About Me

I'm Akul, a finance student at the University of Toronto with a simple obsession: understanding what a business is actually worth, and building the tools that answer that question faster and more rigorously than doing it by hand.

Most recently, I spent a term at Timbercreek Capital in real estate private equity, where I built LBO, value-add, and development models for acquisitions across Europe and North America, audited fund waterfalls, and helped launch a litigation finance strategy from research to live underwriting. Before that, I worked at CI Financial monitoring risk and performance across $3B+ in multi-asset portfolios. Outside of work, I founded a 60-member investment club and have taken first place five times across valuation, negotiation, trading, and portfolio management competitions.

What sets my work apart is the intersection of finance and code. I write Python engines that pull raw SEC filings and produce complete, live-formula Excel models with validation suites behind them, because I believe the analyst who can automate the mechanics gets to spend more time on the judgment. You can download both my hand-built work and my code-generated output below and see the difference for yourself.

Automated DCF Valuation Engine

PYTHON · SEC EDGAR · EXCEL AUTOMATION

End-to-end DCF pipeline pulling SEC EDGAR companyfacts, Damodaran datasets, Yahoo Finance, and FRED into 10-year forecasts with WACC builds, sensitivity grids, and scenario analysis, all delivered as live-formula Excel workbooks. Includes a 44-check validation suite with SHA-256 provenance fingerprinting so no workbook built on superseded code can validate clean.

PythonXBRLValidation SuiteDCF

LBO / Take-Private Modeller

PYTHON · LIVE-FORMULA WORKBOOKS

Give it a ticker and it builds an eight-tab LBO workbook: Sources & Uses, operating model, tranche-level debt schedule with senior-first cash sweep, IRR/MOIC returns with a value-creation bridge, and sensitivity heat maps. Every cell is a real Excel formula driven by an editable Inputs sheet, verified by recalculating the workbook against the Python engine.

PythonLBODebt SchedulesTested
